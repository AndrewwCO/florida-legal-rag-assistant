Earnest Money Deposit in Florida Real Estate

An earnest money deposit is a payment made by a buyer to demonstrate serious intent
to purchase a property.

This deposit is usually submitted shortly after the purchase agreement is signed.
The money is typically held in an escrow account by a title company, attorney,
or real estate brokerage.

Purpose of Earnest Money:
- Shows good faith from the buyer
- Protects the seller if the buyer backs out without valid reason

If the transaction closes successfully, the earnest money deposit is usually applied
toward the purchase price or closing costs.

If the buyer cancels the contract without a valid contingency, the seller may be
entitled to keep the earnest money deposit.
