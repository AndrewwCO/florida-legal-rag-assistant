Inspection Period in Florida Real Estate Contracts

The inspection period allows the buyer to examine the condition of the property
before completing the purchase.

In Florida, this period typically lasts between 7 and 15 days depending on the
terms specified in the purchase agreement.

During the inspection period the buyer may:

- Hire professional home inspectors
- Evaluate structural, electrical, and plumbing conditions
- Request repairs from the seller
- Cancel the contract without penalty if major defects are discovered

If the buyer does not cancel during this period, the contract normally continues
toward closing.
