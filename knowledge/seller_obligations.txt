Seller Obligations in Florida Real Estate

Sellers in Florida must follow certain legal obligations when selling
a residential property.

One important responsibility is the disclosure of known property defects.

Sellers must inform potential buyers about significant issues that could
affect the value or safety of the property.

Examples include:

- Structural damage
- Roof leaks
- Plumbing problems
- Electrical hazards

Failure to disclose known defects may lead to legal liability for the seller
after the sale is completed.
