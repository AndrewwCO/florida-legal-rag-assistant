Rights of Buyers in Florida Real Estate Transactions

Buyers in Florida real estate transactions have several legal rights designed
to protect them during the home purchasing process.

Some important buyer rights include:

- The right to inspect the property before purchase
- The right to review the purchase agreement before signing
- The right to receive accurate property disclosures
- The right to cancel the contract under certain contingencies

Buyers are encouraged to work with licensed real estate agents and attorneys
to fully understand their contractual rights and obligations.
