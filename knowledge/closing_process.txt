Real Estate Closing Process

Closing is the final step in a real estate transaction where ownership of the
property is officially transferred from the seller to the buyer.

During closing several important actions occur:

- The buyer pays the remaining balance of the purchase price
- The seller transfers the property title
- Legal documents are signed by both parties
- Closing costs and taxes are paid

Closing typically takes place at a title company, attorney's office,
or closing agent's office.

After closing is completed, the buyer becomes the legal owner of the property.
