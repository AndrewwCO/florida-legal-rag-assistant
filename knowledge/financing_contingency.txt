Financing Contingency in Real Estate Contracts

A financing contingency is a clause in a real estate purchase agreement that
protects the buyer if they cannot obtain a mortgage loan.

This contingency allows the buyer to cancel the contract without penalty
if the loan application is denied by the lender.

Key aspects of financing contingency:

- The buyer must apply for a mortgage within a specified period
- The lender evaluates the buyer's financial situation
- If financing is not approved, the buyer may terminate the contract

Without this contingency, a buyer who fails to secure financing may lose
their earnest money deposit.
